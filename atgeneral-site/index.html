One system, two front doors

Clubhouse runs the club. Sideline is the club, in everyone's pocket.

Add a player once and they exist everywhere — on the roster, in the squad picker, on the subs ledger, and in their own account. Nothing gets typed twice.

Clubhouse — for the committee

The control room for whoever runs the club

Built for the chairman, treasurer, and coaches — the people who need to know who's paid, who's playing, and who's owed a jersey back.

  • 01
    Roster & complianceEvery player, every age group, and a plain green/amber/red read on who's up to date.
  • 02
    Matchday squad builderPick a line-up and subs from the roster, then print an official, ready-to-sign team sheet.
  • 03
    Subs & payments ledgerA running record of who's paid what, without a shared spreadsheet or a shoebox of receipts.
  • 04
    Kit & stockTrack what's in the store room and what's out with players, updated as items are issued and returned.
  • 05
    Fixtures & noticesImport a season's fixtures, edit by hand when plans change, and post one notice instead of four messages.

Sideline — for players & parents

Everything a player or parent needs, nothing they don't

A pocket-sized view of the club: what's on this week, what's owed, and what's been announced — no login for the coach required.

  • 01
    My fixturesWhat's on, when, and where — filtered to their own team automatically.
  • 02
    Pay in a tapSettle subs online, no cash envelope or bank reference to remember.
  • 03
    Club noticesAnnouncements and training changes, targeted to the right team and read once — not retyped into a group chat.
  • 04
    One account, every kidParents with more than one child in the club see all of them from a single login.
  • 05
    LoyaltyA simple way to recognise players and volunteers who show up, season after season.
